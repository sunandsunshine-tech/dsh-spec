# Historical simplification patterns

Read a section's judgement first. The cases under it are the recorded material the judgement was drawn from, and they belong to the project this pattern was extracted from: replace them with your own project's cases as you accumulate them, and keep the judgement. A case is evidence for a judgement, never a current removal opportunity, so verify current owners and released-data obligations before applying one, and read a project's own record for a candidate rather than a summary of it.

Each section has three levels: the judgement, the forces it weighs and what would reverse it, and the cases.

## Require a complete effect path

**The judgement.** A declaration with no producer, no reader, or only implementations that reject it is contract surface rather than intent: every implementation and consumer has to consider it, and it returns nothing. Remove it until something emits it.

**What it weighs, and what flips it.** The maintenance weight of an admitted slot — a vocabulary member every adapter, renderer and consumer must decide about — against the consumer evidence, which is a search for constructors and discriminators rather than callers. Removing it also upgrades the failure mode: a stray setter becomes a compile error instead of a runtime throw. Zero fixed callers is weaker evidence than unreachability, because a deliberate third-party registration path and a catalog that an out-of-tree plugin mounts are real consumers that no in-repo search finds. What flips it is a producer arriving together with the consumer that routes on it; a field every provider rejects and a working extension point require opposite verdicts.

**The cases.** Reserved union members with no emitter — a cache hint and its optional field on two block types, an `agent` message source with zero constructors, and a turn-trigger `continuation` that structurally cannot be emitted, written only by a hand-built fixture while the one production reader of triggers filters `kind === 'message'`. Request knobs whose only implementation was rejection — a `prefill` option both adapters threw `UNSUPPORTED` for, its entire observable behaviour two throws each pinned by one adapter test, and a `strict` tool-schema flag no shipped tool set while the endpoint that would honour it was a beta feature behind a base URL neither adapter targeted. The counter-case is a runtime skill-registration API with no in-repo caller that was kept, because third-party plugins are its intended consumers.

## Reduce distinctions where consumers decide

**The judgement.** Map every public variant to the action it enables for its consumer. A distinction no consumer can act on is a choice it can get wrong; a distinction that still decides authorization, residency, durability or ownership stays, even when a renderer ignores it.

**What it weighs, and what flips it.** Public correctness — two ways to address the same thing invite the consumer to pick the unusable one — against what the internal distinction still decides: the identity a durable record, an authorization check or a delivery path resolves through, and whether delivery starts something already loaded or restores it from storage. The accepted loss is recorded as a loss: a consumer that no longer receives the second identifier or the finer status has to ask for it, and the note has to say that nothing it does needs it. What flips it is any consumer that needs the second address, or an internal distinction that stops deciding behaviour.

**The cases.** A team tool returned both member names and session identifiers, which invited an unusable address; it returns the name and omits the identifiers, while internal services, views and persisted records keep the session identity they authorize with. Loaded-between-turns and stored agents had separate statuses although continuing either takes the same action: they project as one inactive state, only an executing turn is running, and the states that carry real news — provisioning, failure, a read-error reason — keep their own names. Two approximate capability registries that were intersected, ordered and invalidated on every lifecycle change were replaced by one capability-neutral statement derived from the request's own resolution, with truthfulness kept by conditioning the wording on the enforced mode and the schemas left as the authority; the combinatorial tests reflected that design's cost rather than a requirement. The counter-case is a durable step boundary kept although the turn and step fields already accompany every event, because without it a reader cannot tell a finished model request from a crashed one except by deriving it from the next event.

## Trade general behavior for a smaller explicit promise

**The judgement.** A used feature can still be an expensive way to satisfy the required outcome. The question is not whether the generality is used but what it costs to keep, and the loss is stated as clearly as the deletion.

**What it weighs, and what flips it.** The machinery the general version owns — parsers, evaluators, durable record variants, cross-record admission gates and the states that report their exhaustion — against the behaviour actually asked for, with three forces deciding the narrow replacement. Correctness: anchoring the narrow version on the moment the record was created keeps its meaning under catch-up, where advancing from the moment of delivery turns a fixed rate into a drifting delay. Bounded work: replaying every missed occurrence grows with downtime, and one shared catch-up batch bounds the work a set of overdue records costs. Reversibility: pre-release records are rejected by the strict decoder rather than migrated, which is a deliberate loss of old data. What flips it is a product boundary that needs the generality rather than dormant compatibility for it, or evidence that the narrower promise drops required data.

**The cases.** Calendar recurrence and missed-occurrence replay for durable reminders, where only a fixed interval had been asked for: a cron language and evaluator, zone-sensitive occurrence search, a cross-record 300-second admission gate with persisted evidence, deferred-delivery fields and gate-exhaustion states all went, while the interval's meaning under catch-up was kept by anchoring the first target at creation and advancing to the next aligned one. Automatic rollback of a failed configuration reload, whose candidate imports, lifecycle settlement, rollback and option identity made the vendored copy diverge from its pins: it went, application consumers audit activation themselves, and a plugin failure that leaves a partially applied tree is the documented cost. The counter-case is truncating an interrupted turn, rejected because one turn can hold substantial real work — assistant text, tool calls, tool output — and the synthetic repair that invents events is the cheaper of the two losses while the log is the durable replay and audit surface.

## Make assumptions explicit at their consuming operation

**The judgement.** A convenience assumption kept where it is consumed costs nothing shared. The same assumption stored as shared state acquires owners, synchronization and confirmation machinery, so move the requirement to the operation that must enforce it and leave the source fact on the request that carries it.

**What it weighs, and what flips it.** Ownership first: a durable default duplicates an assumption instead of strengthening the boundary, and most of the complexity sits in the layers that were only forwarding it. State correctness next: an immutable persisted default still needs mismatch handling for travel, concurrent clients and old records, and a mutable most-recent value lets one client silently change another's interpretation and makes replay depend on update order. Then the seam — a prose snapshot is model-visible evidence, not a typed package seam — and usability, because asking on every unqualified input is safe and interrupts the common case. Where the assumption is a validation rather than a default, the same rule reads as: keep it at the boundary that must enforce it anyway, and record what the earlier local failure was buying. What flips it is a consumer that cannot supply the fact, or a requirement for interpretation-free determinism.

**The cases.** A browser time zone captured as a session default, which required headers, create/resume/fork conflict rules, a log field and a storage migration plus a confirmation protocol for travel and concurrent tabs: keeping the zone on the request — an optional field validated at the RPC boundary and logged on that exact message — deleted all of it, left the configured zone as a display fallback that is never presented as the user's authority, and made the scheduling tool accept only an explicit offset-bearing timestamp or an exact date-and-zone. Injected content wrapped in XML envelopes to mark it as not the user speaking: no model is trained on those tags, a recorded transcript shows a model treating one as third-party metadata and refusing it while answering only the original prompt, and the heaviest producer had already opted out. The transcript went back to a verbatim projection and framing became the producer's business. The counter-case is duplicate client-side schema execution removed while the host keeps validating before lookup and invocation, with the cost recorded — undeclared properties can cross the carrier and malformed JavaScript values fail later — and codec metadata retained because the descriptor is shared.

## Derive at use or reuse an existing delivery owner

**The judgement.** For every cache, copied history or change notification, ask when the consumer needs the value and whether an authoritative source already retains it. Reading the authority at the moment of use, or reusing the stream that already publishes it, deletes the copy and everything that kept it consistent.

**What it weighs, and what flips it.** The timing promise decides whether the derivation is available: redaction at feedback time is not redaction at capture time, manual placement cannot be derived from activity timestamps, and an initial historical read may remain necessary where a baseline can be absent. Durability decides what the fallback has to be, because a cache hint may legitimately be absent and the reader then needs a complete read rather than a delta. Data integrity decides the shape of the reuse: letting a late local response overwrite newer host state generates a false completion. What flips it is state no timestamp expresses, an ordering the authoritative source cannot reproduce, or an observation required earlier than the trigger that would read it.

**The cases.** Feedback-only telemetry that deep-copied and redacted every projected event until feedback arrived, unbounded for a session that never recorded any: reading the canonical log at the trigger through a cursor replaced it, at the cost that a crash before feedback uploads nothing and a previous lifecycle's prefix is not released, which is why a durable spool is deferred until a deployment needs crash recovery before feedback. A sidebar order promoted by activity that could contradict its own displayed timestamps when an older session arrived late: the order became a projection of the current summaries with the manual layout kept as persisted display state, and entering or leaving the manual mode now freezes or discards a layout instead of reconciling one. A parent catalog whose complete membership already arrived on the session control stream, duplicated by a change event, repeated reads and a second cache: the duplicates went, and the client keeps membership only in its per-session projection store, with a complete initial read deferred while historical sessions can lack a projection.

## Remove accidental composition owners

**The judgement.** A name such as demo, support or example is not evidence about what a thing is. Inspect the actual launch and resolution paths: a package that exists to make resolution convenient, a launcher that looks like a product interface, or a service every caller uses only in combination with another is composition that can go, while the distinction it carried survives as configuration or as an internal owner.

**What it weighs, and what flips it.** Ownership — paths that identify an owner without consulting a verdict table, against an umbrella manifest that hides ownership and lets an arbitrary leaf behave like an application package. Maintenance weight — the same rows maintained twice, or two public services composed by every real caller — against what the merged owner must keep: a durable identifier allocated synchronously, a completion listener, teardown effects. Policy against capability: sibling presence states that something can be resumed, not how a deployment intends it to run, so the intent becomes an explicit option. Mechanism constraints bound the fix, because a patch that cannot cross an include boundary or create a row makes a personal overlay a silent no-op, and what the mechanism cannot express stays in the base. A smaller package count is not sufficient evidence on its own: separate caller intents can differ in ownership and completion timing, and a retained service definition may serve independent providers. What flips it is a composition that resolves modules without hiding ownership, or a second vocabulary for a launch the narrower one cannot express.

**The cases.** Two configuration trees that kept 43 rows the same, one of them a launcher whose single row mounted twelve plugins and re-declared their settings as its own pass-through block: a shared base plus per-surface patch overlays replaced them, with the five rows that genuinely differ per surface left in the base because a patch cannot create a row, and composition verified by booting each tree through the real loader — which surfaced three latent defects the duplicated trees had been hiding. A top-level examples tree that mixed redundant application compositions, cross-package profile tests, loader fixtures and user guides, its umbrella manifest existing mainly so nested configuration files resolved their packages: every file moved beside its owner, and the workspace, which was kept for the resolver rather than for a product, disappeared with the demo launchers that had looked like interfaces. A continuable-child control service above the raw provider contract, composed by every real caller: one public service now carries start, start-continuable and follow-up, the provider dispatch is private to the continuation manager, and the background mode is an explicit option instead of being inferred from whether the provider and a sibling tool happened to be loaded. The counter-case is optional dependencies inside the same design, which stayed, and a retained service definition that serves independent providers, which was not a duplicate.

## Use rejected experiments to test parity claims

**The judgement.** A rejection is a scoped falsifier with a recorded reason, not a policy and not a blacklist. A later proposal has to beat that reason, and the measurement includes the implementation, the glue, the tests, the dependencies and the behaviour the replacement cannot express.

**What it weighs, and what flips it.** Implementation cost on both sides — what is deleted against what the replacement does not implement, which is where configured bounds, security rules, lifecycle ownership and deployment behaviour usually sit. The test harness, which is decisive more often than the deleted lines: a nominally equivalent builtin that the fake clock does not intercept costs the deterministic fast tests the hand-rolled helper kept passing. Supply-chain surface, where a de-facto standard already present transitively adds none and a safety boundary is the wrong place to save one line. Contract fidelity — a wire format that must stay bit-identical for a client the project does not own — and the robustness given up, such as a spec-strict parser that rejects a trailing terminator the hand-rolled flush accepted. What flips it is new evidence addressing the premise that failed: the harness grows support, the library learns to express the bound while keeping the teardown semantics, the deleted lines come to outweigh the lost coverage. Recording nothing is not the alternative, because a pull request's own description is not part of the maintained record.

**The cases.** A hand-written SSE parser of about 67 lines with about 108 lines of conformance tests, replaced by a maintained stream parser that left only the provider shim: the spec tests went, the robustness given up is pinned as a stricter truncated-tail verdict, and the library had the margin of already being present in the lockfile transitively. Replacing three hand-rolled promise-wrapped delays, about ten lines in total, with the builtin was rejected after the swap was implemented and failed its own acceptance criterion: the test harness's fake clock does not intercept the builtin, so a small consistency win cost deterministic fast tests. A repository-wide audit that asked whether a maintained package or a builtin could delete each hand-rolled surface froze roughly thirty negative verdicts with their reasons — a serializer queue of eight to fourteen lines behind a package strictly larger than the code it would delete, a quoting helper on a safety boundary, a JSON-RPC wire that must stay bit-identical for a hand-rolled client, a SQLite binding that gates the engine floor — so a later proposal for any of them has to beat the recorded reason rather than re-cite the policy.
