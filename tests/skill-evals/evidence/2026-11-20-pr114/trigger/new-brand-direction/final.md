These are three category-agnostic identity systems designed to stay distinct after they expand beyond the hero. Each includes landing-page structure, typography, color roles, graphic language, and conversion treatment.

| Direction | Brand feeling | Typography | Best fit |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Switchyard** | Controlled momentum | Archivo Black · Space Grotesk · IBM Plex Mono | Technical or infrastructure products |
| **Commonplace** | Considered authority | Instrument Serif · Source Sans 3 · IBM Plex Mono | Collaboration, insight, or decision products |
| **Afterglow** | Expressive optimism | Bricolage Grotesque · DM Sans · IBM Plex Mono | Creative tools or high-visibility launches |

## 1. Switchyard

A transit-signage system crossed with an instrument panel. It communicates speed, precision, and forward motion without looking like a generic developer dashboard.

**Palette**

- Off-white `#F4F1E8` — canvas
- Graphite `#121515` — typography and dark rails
- Steel `#4C5353` — secondary copy and rules
- Safety Orange `#FF5A1F` — conversions and route markers
- Signal Lime `#C6FF3D` — live states and directional accents

Graphite on off-white reaches `16.3:1`; graphite on orange reaches `5.9:1`, so the primary CTA remains accessible.

**Typography**

- Hero: Archivo Black, 88–168px, tight tracking, short three-line statements
- Interface and subheads: Space Grotesk 400/600
- Labels and route codes: IBM Plex Mono 500 at 12px and above

**Landing-page identity**

Use an asymmetric 60/40 hero. The headline is cropped slightly at the left edge; the right side is a tall graphite routing rail with one abstract path, numbered waypoints, and a single lime state marker. Continue that route as a 12px line through the rest of the page, turning each section into a waypoint.

The logo device is a switched arrow: two parallel rails interrupted by a 45-degree change of direction. Keep corners square or nearly square, rules at 1–2px, and motion fast and functional at 140–220ms.

**Suggested copy**

> Change direction.
> Keep the thread.

One clear route from first signal to next move.

Primary CTA: **Choose the route →**

**Risk to manage:** Avoid walls of equally sized metrics. Use one dominant route diagram so the page feels directed rather than instrument-dense.

## 2. Commonplace

A living record: part editorial archive, part modern operating system. It makes the brand feel thoughtful and authoritative while avoiding the familiar beige startup aesthetic.

**Palette**

- Parchment `#F2EBDD` — canvas
- Ink `#18201D` — primary typography
- Slate `#4F5B55` — secondary copy
- Oxblood `#8C2F39` — primary actions
- Cobalt `#1D3E8A` — links and focus states
- Chartreuse `#D8E55C` — annotations and highlights
- Chalk `#FFF8EC` — inverse text

Ink on parchment reaches `14:1`; chalk on oxblood reaches `7.7:1`; slate on parchment reaches `6:1`.

**Typography**

- Hero: Instrument Serif Regular and Italic, 96–132px, `0.92` line-height
- Body and interface: Source Sans 3 400/600
- Labels and indices: IBM Plex Mono 400/500 at 12px and above

**Landing-page identity**

Build the hero on a 12-column editorial grid. Give the serif headline roughly eight columns, place an index-card-style product detail beside it, and reserve one narrow column for footnotes and metadata. A fine shelf line below the copy carries the CTA and supporting proof.

Use registration crosses, ruler ticks, issue numbers, bracket corners, and occasional chartreuse highlighter strokes. The logo mark can be a small registration target beside a serif wordmark. Product evidence should be treated like documentary material—captioned, cropped, and clearly labeled.

**Suggested copy**

> The work deserves
> a clearer record.

Turn moving parts into one story people can follow.

Primary CTA: **Begin the record →**

**Risk to manage:** The warm palette needs crisp typography, concrete product proof, and disciplined highlighter use. Otherwise it can drift into editorial styling without enough substance.

## 3. Afterglow

A color-field poster translated into a digital product. It introduces emotional range and memorability while retaining enough geometry to function as a product brand.

**Palette**

- Chalk `#FFF8E8` — canvas
- Ink `#20211F` — primary typography
- Slate `#5C5D57` — secondary copy
- Iris `#C8B6FF` — atmospheric fields
- Solar `#FF6B35` — actions and signature accents
- Marine `#2457E6` — inverse panels and offset layers

Ink on chalk reaches `15.3:1`; ink on solar reaches `5.7:1`; chalk on marine reaches `5.5:1`. Keep orange for large actions and graphic fields rather than small text.

**Typography**

- Hero: Bricolage Grotesque 600, 80–144px
- Body and controls: DM Sans 400/600
- Captions and indices: IBM Plex Mono 400

**Landing-page identity**

Place a three-line headline in the open center of an oversized concentric aperture cropped by the top and right edges. Attach a narrow marine caption rail at the far right. The aperture becomes the repeating identity device: section transition, loading mark, numbered waypoint, and occasionally a portrait or product-image mask.

Use sharply registered color fields and a solid offset second impression. Avoid blurred gradients. The primary CTA can be two stacked planes—solar over marine—with the arrow advancing on hover.

**Suggested copy**

> Make room
> for a brighter
> kind of useful.

Shape raw possibility into a next step people can move with.

Primary CTA: **Step into the open →**

**Risk to manage:** The visual field can overpower the message. Keep chromatic areas large and flat, limit the page to three dominant colors, and preserve generous neutral space.

## Recommendation

Start with **Commonplace**. It is the most ownable system and the least dependent on current software-landing-page conventions. Instrument Serif supplies an immediate point of view, the index-card structure creates a distinctive content system, and the oxblood/cobalt pairing gives it usability and conversion clarity.

Choose **Switchyard** instead if the product is developer-facing or infrastructure-led. Choose **Afterglow** if the launch needs maximum visual memorability.

Across all three, retain 16px minimum body text, 44×44px targets, visible focus rings, textual labels alongside color states, and a reduced-motion version of every transition.
