No installed skill fits visual-direction generation, so here are three concise options.

| Direction | Palette | Typography | Landing-page identity |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Signal** — precise, intelligent, future-facing | Ink `#0B132B`, cyan `#35D5C7`, off-white `#F5F7F4` | Geist for UI and headlines; IBM Plex Mono for labels | Modular 12-column grid, thin technical rules, data-inspired diagrams, product imagery in crisp browser frames, restrained scanning animation, compact sentence-case wordmark |
| **Common Ground** — warm, editorial, human | Oat `#F2E8D5`, terracotta `#C65D3B`, forest `#20342B` | Instrument Serif for headlines; DM Sans for body | Generous editorial spacing, large portraits or real-work photography, organic cut-paper arcs, hand-drawn underlines, tactile grain, inviting serif wordmark |
| **Voltage** — energetic, expressive, memorable | Charcoal `#111111`, orange `#FF4D00`, butter `#FFD84D` | Archivo Black for headlines; Space Mono for details | Oversized cropped type, high-contrast panels, offset image cards, bold ticker strips, strong diagonal motion, mechanically spaced uppercase wordmark |

For all three, the landing page should use one dominant idea above the fold, one clear CTA, real product or human imagery, and a consistent illustration or motion language rather than mixing styles.
